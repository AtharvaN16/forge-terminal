A key that is down

Held fixed from round one: pressed and on look the same, and only the key changes — the words beside it stay as they are at rest. Which look for the key? Each concept shows the real home with Tab pressed, and the eight kinds of key at rest and down. “/ menu” keeps its gold in all of them; the field chip goes white; the no-colour key is reversed, except in F. Dark, 80 × 24. Round one’s page is unchanged.
