Transcribe: progress, clock, format

Three open questions, each on its own tab: how “Writing notes” sits under “Transcribing”, how the clock says minutes, and where the output format is chosen. Screens are the real app at 80 × 24, dark; only the rows in question are redrawn. The middle of each screen is cut (dashed line) to keep them short.

Decided — from your message

  • “Writing notes” is its own row, under “Transcribing”.
  • Squares, not dots, for the parts.
  • The clock says minutes once it passes 60 seconds; under a minute it stays in seconds.
  • A choice of output format: Markdown (today), Word, PDF, text.

Questions for you

  1. New words on screen, to approve: the step name Format; the names Markdown · Word · PDF · Text with .md · .docx · .pdf · .txt; “2 of 5 parts” under the squares; F2’s “tab format”; F4’s “e export” and “Export a copy”.
  2. Should the format be remembered between runs? (Detail is not.) My pick: yes — a format is a habit, Detail is per talk.
  3. The clock (tab 2) is shared by every Working screen. Change it everywhere? My pick: yes.
  4. Word and PDF: Word would come from macOS’s own textutil (no download). PDF needs a renderer — the Swift helper already in Forge can print one. Text is the Markdown with its marks removed. OK to have Word and PDF look plain (headings, bullets, times, no styling)?
