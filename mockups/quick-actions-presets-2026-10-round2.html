P1One row, ↵ opens them all

Your idea. The box keeps one row however many presets you have — how many are in Finder. ↵ opens a window over it with all of them; ↵ turns the lit one on or off; esc closes.

P2The box shows Finder’s menu; a pop-up adds

My pick. The box lists only the presets in Finder — the same list you see on right-click — and ends in + Add a preset, which opens a window of the rest. A long list of presets never reaches the box; only a long Finder menu would, and you’d see that here.
