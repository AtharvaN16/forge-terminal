Transcribe · round 2

Transcribe as its own mode on home: drop a video or sound file, type a path, or paste a YouTube link; get notes or a transcript. Home and Save as are captured from the app at 80 × 24 and recoloured; the rest is drawn with the app’s own art functions. The file is the spike’s real output from a one-hour talk.

OPEN · ROUND 2

Decided

  1. Transcribe is its own mode: the sixth tab on home, in red, in the slot “/ menu” had.
  2. “/ menu” sits above the key row, right-aligned.
  3. Done: a green “done” chip, the file and its parts; ↵ new, o open, s finder, c copy on the key row. No word count.
  4. A path, a dropped file or a YouTube link, then the flow goes on from there.
  5. Mac only (macOS 26 or later). Windows only if it has equivalent built-in tools.
  6. Apple does the work: its speech recogniser, and Apple Intelligence for the notes.
  7. The file says the notes may be wrong, in one line under the summary.

Needs you

  1. YouTube’s terms do not allow downloading except through their own buttons. Many tools do it anyway (yt-dlp itself, MacWhisper), and the add-on keeps yt-dlp out of Forge’s own package, but it is a risk only you can take on. Keep the YouTube link, or drop it and keep files and paths only?
