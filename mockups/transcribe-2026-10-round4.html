Transcribe: Working decided, format split

The Working screen as decided, then one open question: how the format sits on the Transcribe to screen. Real app cells at 80 × 24, dark; the short window is the same screen at 80 × 16.

Decided

  • Working: Transcribing keeps its line; when it is done the bar goes and it reads “✓ Transcribing 1m 08s”. Writing notes has the spinner and W3’s squares.
  • Clock: T1 — “42.3s” under a minute, then “4m 12s”, “1h 03m”. Every Working screen.
  • No “nothing is saved until it is done” on the key row. My call: gone from every Working screen, not only transcribe’s — tell me if one should keep it.
  • Format: on the same screen as Transcribe to, ↑↓ only, no tab. One format can be made the default.

Questions for you

  1. Making a format the default: tab make default, as Save as does for folders, and the same amber default tag. OK?
  2. The list opens on the default format, so a habit costs no keys.
  3. New words on screen, to approve: Format, Markdown · Word · PDF · Text, .md · .docx · .pdf · .txt, “←→ format” (S2, S4).
