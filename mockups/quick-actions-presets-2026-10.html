Presets in Finder’s Quick Actions

You choose which presets appear in Finder’s right-click menu, in /right-click. Terminal screens are the real app at 80 × 24; Finder is drawn.

1Off until you turn it on

Presets are chosen in /right-click. Saving a preset never touches Finder.

2Runs on its own

Like Sharpen in Forge: no window, one notification, the new file selected.

3Follows your presets

Rename or delete one and its item follows. Forge off hides them all; they come back as you left them.
