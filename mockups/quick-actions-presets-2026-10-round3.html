Uninstalling Forge — in a frame

The section under the presets in /right-click, six ways. Terminal screens are the real /right-click at 80 × 24, edited.

The question

How should “Uninstalling Forge” be framed? Six different looks, the decided words unchanged.

Drawn on P2 (round two) with one preset in Finder; the frame works the same on P1. At 80 × 24 the box has 17 rows, so a frame costs two of them — with many presets in the box, Uninstalling moves out of view and scrolls.
