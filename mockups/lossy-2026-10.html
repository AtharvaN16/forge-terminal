Lossy PNG and GIF

/compress on a PNG or GIF makes it smaller by using fewer colours (PNG) and reusing unchanged pixels between frames (GIF). /convert to PNG stays perfect quality. Screens are captured from the app at 80 × 24; sizes are measured.

Ideas that go further than Clop
  • Dithering chosen per image — tab 4. Clop always dithers.
  • Never write a bigger file — tab 5. Agreed; covers JPEG and WebP too, not only PNG and GIF.

SCREEN — decided: N4

Flat colours by default. At 65 and 30 a note above the keys always offers dithering, with d on the same grey cap as every key below it and lined up with them. Press d and the note says “Dithering on”; the band stays as it is. At 100 the note shows only when dithering changes the file (a gradient still bands at 100; your screenshot comes out the same either way). It never shows when the two copies come out identical — logos and icons with few colours, where d would do nothing. Forge does not judge the look: you press d and see. Sizes measured on your screenshot: 65 is 123 KB flat, 152 KB dithered; 30 is 107 KB, 124 KB.

SCREEN — decided: E3

A PNG another tool already squeezed (screenshot-small.png, 167 KB) would come out at 168 KB at the 100 point. Forge never writes a bigger file. Applies to every format /compress takes — a JPEG saved at 60 and compressed at 90 grows 49 → 74 KB today.
