Presets and Continue editing, round two

Presets live everywhere you said; this round is how they look in each place, a few genuinely different shapes for Continue editing, bolder ways to show a step working, and the leaving question asked inside the window. Captured from the app, dark, 80 × 24 unless a screen says otherwise.

Decided in round one

  • Presets live everywhere offered: the / menu (/webblog), the mode’s own picker when a preset applies (convert, compress, pdf…), home’s field once a file or folder is staged, and Finder’s Quick Actions.
  • No space to select. Rows are chosen with ↵, and the lit row carries its button (the field key chip, in the mode’s colour).
  • Asked on leaving (D3), with the answers inside the window.
  • Gone: a presets tab on home.

P1A section above the formats (round one’s A2)Liked

As liked in round one: presets first, a rule, the formats. Three presets cost four rows, so at 20 rows the last formats scroll off (↓ 3 more). Video: the section sits above the two boxes.

P2Split: a presets box beside the formats

Your split, in the video picker’s own boxes: ←→ between boxes, ↑↓ in one. The lit preset’s steps on the detail line under the boxes, where video names its codec. A photo’s six formats make the boxes eight rows tall, so it needs 22 rows: at 20 the boxes’ foot and the detail line scroll off. A video (four rows of formats) fits, with a third box.

P3Two tabs: formats · presets

The title row holds two tabs; tab switches the list. Never taller than today, whatever the count — but the presets are one key away, so they are easy to miss.

P4Pills on the title’s row

MY PICK. The presets sit at the right of the title row, which is empty today, so no row is added at any height. ↑ from the first format lights them; the lit one’s steps and size show on the row under, also empty today. Same in a video’s picker and compress’s Method.

P5Split: a column, as edit’s picker is

Formats on the left as today, presets in a column right of a │ — the shape edit’s picker already has. No rows added. A video’s two boxes leave no room for a column, so a video would fall back to P4.

OPTIONS — the presets that fit the staged file, in or by home’s field; ↵ stays where it is

H1The field grows down

MY PICK. The presets that apply open on the field’s shelf, under the field. ↓ moves into them; the chip keeps its place and the key row says what ↵ will do. A folder: each preset says how many of its files it applies to.

H2Pills in the field’s row

No row added: the presets as pills just before ↵ enter; tab moves along them, the lit one’s steps appear under it. Tight once names are long or there are many.

H3On the file’s card instead

Not in the field: the card in the drop zone grows a row of pills, so the presets read as being about this file. tab moves along them.

OPTIONS — different shapes for carrying on from Done. No space to select: ↵ and a button on the lit row

K1It is edit mode, carried on

MY PICK. → opens edit’s own picker on the file just made — the screen people already know — with the steps so far at the top right. Each operation runs its own screen and comes back here with a ✓. Done (first row) keeps the file; p saves the steps.

K2A steps screen with sizes

A full screen, not a window: each step a row, with the size it leaves and a bar for that size — what each step bought you. ↵ changes the lit step, ⌫ removes it, + adds one.

K3A menu, like the / menu

A small popup from Done’s bottom right, in the / menu’s manner: your steps, then what you can add, ↵ add on the lit row. Small and familiar, but Done stays behind it.

K4Goals, not operations

Plain wants with ready answers — Smaller file (under 300 KB), Web size (1920 wide), Square (1:1)… ↵ adds one with its answer, → changes the answer. Something else opens the full list.

K5Today’s window, with buttons

For comparison: W2 as built, space gone — ✓ marks your steps, the lit row carries ↵ add or ↵ remove, esc is done.

OPTIONS — Compress on a 16 MB photo takes seconds: what does the window show? Every number here is one Forge really has

R1The search, drawn

A ruler of sizes from 0 to the file you had: the target a green line, each try a mark where its size landed, the current one spinning. You watch it close in on the line. The rows go quiet; esc stops.

R2Stations

MY PICK. Your steps as a line — done ✓, working ⠹, waiting ○ — ending in the file. Under the working one, what it is doing right now. Works for any step, not only a size search.

R3The working screen everyone knows

The window steps aside for the same working screen every convert shows — the card, one line, the clock, the header’s hatching drifting — then comes back when the step lands. Nothing new to learn.

R4The picture, shimmering

The photo itself in the window, a light passing over it while the new one is made; beside it, before → … and what is happening. Half blocks may seam in Apple Terminal.

R5A log

Each try printed as a line, as a terminal would: quality, size, too big or fits. Honest and plain; long searches scroll.
