Video to notes · round 1

Convert a video or sound file into a written file: the transcript, and notes when Apple Intelligence is on. Home and Save as are captured from the app at 80 × 24; the rest is drawn with the app’s own art functions. The file previews are the spike’s real output from a one-hour talk.

OPEN · ROUND 1

Decided

  1. Mac only (macOS 26 or later). Windows only if it has equivalent built-in tools.
  2. Apple does the work: its speech recogniser for the transcript, Apple Intelligence for the notes. Forge adds about 0.3 MB; macOS fetches the speech model itself (about 200 MB a language) when it is not there yet.
  3. The file says the notes may be wrong, in one line under the summary.
  4. No YouTube. Download the video yourself, then drop the file in.
  5. The transcript always works; notes only when Apple Intelligence is on.

My calls · say if any is wrong

  1. F2: Notes, Transcript and Subtitles are three formats in a third box, text. The choice is the format, as everywhere else in Forge, so there is no extra step. When Apple Intelligence is off, Notes is simply not offered.
  2. L1: it listens for the Mac’s own language; another is the CLI’s --language. Most people speak one language, and Apple cannot detect it, so a step would cost everyone a ↵ to serve a few.
  3. D2: Done lists the parts it found with their times. A byte bar (D1) means nothing for text; the headings show at a glance whether it understood the talk.
  4. M1: notes first, then the transcript under the same headings. You read the notes and go to the words only when you need them.
  5. Subtitles (.srt) are in. They come free from the same pass.
  6. A true bar while transcribing, a dot per part while writing notes. Both are real counts; this follows whatever is decided for video’s bar.
  7. The disclaimer: “Summary and notes by Apple Intelligence. They may contain mistakes.” It is short and covers both the summary and the notes.
  8. A part Apple will not write notes for keeps its transcript and its time, and Done says so in one line (Edge cases).
