Video and sound · round 3

Video and sound work exactly like images: convert changes the format, compress makes it smaller, edit cuts or mutes. Home, Method, the size field and Save as are captured from the app at 80 × 24; the rest is drawn with the app’s own art functions.

OPEN · ROUND 3

Decided

  1. The mode is the job. Convert changes the format, compress makes it smaller, edit cuts or mutes. A video goes where an image would.
  2. Forge picks the technical things: codec, frame rate, the picture size needed to fit, HDR, copy or re-encode.
  3. Extras only on the command line: speed, frame rate, bitrate, loudness level, saving a frame.
  4. Two jobs = two runs: Done’s c key sends the result into the next mode.
  5. Installed in place, as Boost is. Trim: filled bar, sound as peaks; I and O for start and end, space for the range, a step picker.
