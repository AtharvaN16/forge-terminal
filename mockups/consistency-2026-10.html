Consistency: today, and with the decided parts

Every screen the app still draws the old way. On the left, the screen as the app draws it today, captured from the real app at 80 × 24 (on dev, 2026-10-11, the neutral greys already in). On the right, the same screen built only from parts already decided. Where a screen needs a design that has not been chosen, its note says so.

The decided parts, and what each older screen is missing.
  • Title. The question in bold, in the mode's colour, with what it is about quiet at its right (Quality, Save as). Older screens use a label that is not bold and has nothing at its right; some are white (Format, Method), some in the mode's colour (Pages, Resolution).
  • Keys (F1). Main keys as caps on the left, a quiet footnote on the right ending esc back · ctrl-c quit. Older screens use the old row of caps, with esc worded five different ways and ctrl-c left out or called "close app".
  • Lists. The picker's band and strip, the band no wider than the file card and in grey (the decided Format picker). The app draws the band in the mode's tint; /pdf's run the whole terminal; merge's uses ❯ on a fill.
  • Fields. Save as's filled field, the block's width, filled at every width. Older fields run the whole terminal and drop the fill when it is narrow.
  • Default. P1's tinted pill at the far end of the row. Resolution says "default" as a hint.
  • Name taken. L1, one version everywhere. /pdf has its own, with a red ✕ heading and no Keep both.

The key row keeps the rule across the terminal above it, as the app draws it today. The app draws the two straight under the screen; the mockups pin them to the terminal's bottom rows. That difference is the mockup kit's, not one to fix.
