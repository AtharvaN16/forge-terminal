People get lost because colour mode shows everything at once.

Six problems, most important first. Screens are from the real app.

1Two cards sound the same

11

Scale and Palette both say “from one colour”. If you have one colour, which do you pick?

2Too much on one screen

  3. 3Technical numbers: oklch, 3.6:1, AA, AAA.

6You have to start over

Photo→pick a colour→make a palette from it

The tools don’t connect. To go from Scale to Palette you type the colour again, and going back from Palette throws it away.

Small things

  • “copied 11 swatches”: copied as what?
  • “make centre” is hard to understand.
  • “wrong input” doesn’t show an example of what works.
  • The longest text on the photo screen is a two-line disclaimer.
  • The rainbow header and the green/red marks compete with the colours.

How we fix it

  1. Two ways in: a photo or a colour.
  2. One clear result first. Extras behind one key.
  3. Say where it goes: “copy for Figma”.
  4. Same keys on every screen. All tips in the bottom row.
  5. Move between tools without typing the colour again.
Needs your OK: two of these fixes undo earlier decisions. The three cards become two ways in, and the Tweak box moves out of the middle of the screen.
