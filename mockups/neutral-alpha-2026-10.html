Neutrals

A neutral is a grey scale tinted with the colour’s hue. Its 500 sits at OKLCH lightness 0.55, where every Tailwind grey’s 500 does, not at the colour’s own lightness: a pale yellow would otherwise give a pale grey 500. How much tint is one setting, measured against Tailwind’s greys. Soft on #6579FD lands on Tailwind gray almost exactly.

Alpha

For each solid step: the most see-through colour that looks exactly like it when laid over white. The colour is pushed as far from white as it can go, so the opacity can be as low as possible; it’s rounded up to a byte, then checked to within 1 on every channel. Radix builds its alpha scales this way. These swatches are real transparency in the browser, over a checkerboard.
