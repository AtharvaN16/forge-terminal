Tools you already have — round 2

Two questions, one tab each. Drawn at 100 columns, so /extensions is the split layout; every screen is the real app, captured, with only the changed cells redrawn.

What this is

  • Forge needs a few outside tools. When your Mac already has one, Forge should use yours instead of downloading a second copy.
  • Which tools this can apply to: ffmpeg (video and audio), yt-dlp (YouTube links), LibreOffice (Word documents), and later Ghostscript (PDF). Boost resolution and Remove background are Forge’s own — nothing on a Mac can stand in for them, so they stay downloads.
  • Today: ffmpeg and LibreOffice are already used from your Mac, silently. yt-dlp is always downloaded.

OPTIONS — four ways for Forge to use a tool that is already on your Mac. Same for every tool; drawn with yt-dlp. Pick one.

AAsk the first time it’s needed

The first YouTube link finds yt-dlp on your Mac and asks once, on the menu row: use it, or download Forge’s. The answer is kept; later links just go. Same for ffmpeg on the first video.

BPoint Forge to it yourself

Your first idea. Each tool in /extensions gets a second way in beside install: o use one on this Mac. A field opens with the copies Forge found (you can type a path too); ↵ picks one. Nothing is used until you say so.

COne table of every tool

/extensions becomes a table of everything Forge uses — your own and its downloads — with where each comes from and the space it takes. The total at the bottom is only what Forge downloaded.

↓
DInstall looks first

Nothing changes until you press install — in /extensions, or on a YouTube link’s ↵. Then Forge looks before it downloads: found one, ↵ use it, or d download anyway.

M1On the menu row

Your idea. The menu row’s empty left end says what is needed and its size; ↵ install stays on the key row. The second frame is the same rule for a video with no ffmpeg.

No sentence at all: the key says it — ↵ install yt-dlp · 37.1 MB. Fewest words; the card and the menu row stay as they are.
