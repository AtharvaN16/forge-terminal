00What enhance does

Three things to do to an image, from two on-device tools. Free, nothing uploaded, each downloaded the first time it is picked.

1Boost resolution · 14.9 MBReal-ESRGAN, run on this Mac. Sharpen keeps the size and brings back edges; Boost resolution 2× and 4× make the image bigger without the smear of a plain resize, up to 16,384 px and 100 MP. No row is a default: you always choose.
2Remove background · 32 KBAsks macOS (Apple Vision) for the subject and saves it as a PNG with a clear background. Small because the model is part of macOS.

01The picker, every row state

The decided picker: rows are names; only the highlighted row says more, on the band. No row is a default, so there is no ★ and no pill. Installed, not installed, installing, too large, and a HEIC source. "Boost resolution 4×" is the longest name; the band stays the decided 64 cells, the same as convert's.

02Done: X5 ✓ decided

For enhance the payoff is the picture: sharper edges, more pixels, a clean cutout. Before and after side by side; the real pictures where the terminal can draw images, otherwise a close-up at true pixel scale, cut where the result differs most from what you had. No bytes. The header bar's trail ends "Done"; one line says what was done and where it went.

LOOK AT
Scale. Sharpen: one square is one pixel of the file (1:1, both sides the same size). Boost resolution 4×: the original's pixels drawn as blocks next to the new pixels, so the blocks are what you had and the smooth edge is what you got (drawn at half the output's size: each old pixel a 2 × 2 block, so a whole word fits; at 1:1 the 4 × 4 blocks hold only part of a letter). Remove background: the subject on a checkerboard.
