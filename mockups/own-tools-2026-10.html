Your own yt-dlp, not a second copy

When your Mac already has yt-dlp, Forge should use it instead of downloading 37.1 MB more — as it already does for ffmpeg. Four moments change; one tab each. Every screen is the real app, captured, with only the changed cells redrawn.

Where this stands

  • ffmpeg already works this way. Forge uses yours (PATH, then Homebrew) and never downloads one. LibreOffice too. Nothing here changes them, unless you like X4.
  • yt-dlp is the only double copy. Today Forge always downloads its own 37.1 MB, even when you have one.
  • Bug found while capturing: on an 80-column window the install card cuts its own line in the middle — YouTube links need yt-dl…B download — so the size never shows. Fixed whatever you pick here.

  6. Small calls I’d make unless you object:
    • Yours wins whenever it is new enough, as ffmpeg does today. “New enough” = at least the version this Forge is tested with.
    • Forge never updates, moves or deletes your copy.
    • The command matches how you installed it: brew upgrade yt-dlp for Homebrew, pipx upgrade yt-dlp, python3 -m pip install -U yt-dlp, else yt-dlp -U.
    • /about’s Attributions lists only what Forge downloaded; your own tools are not Forge’s to credit.

X1Just ✓ ready

The row gets its ✓ like an installed tool; the sheet says ready, with no size and no remove, since nothing of Forge’s is on disk. Quietest; does not say where it comes from.

MY PICK. ✓ on this Mac, its version, and its path under it in grey. Answers “did Forge download this?” at a glance, and the version makes “too old” (tab 3) make sense.

X3The row goes

Nothing to install, so nothing to show: YouTube links leaves the list while your yt-dlp is found. Cleanest list — but nowhere says YouTube links work, or why.

Goes with any of X1–X4. Forge uses yours; its own copy is listed under it, and x frees its 37.1 MB. This is the double-space case, made visible and one key to undo.

OPTIONS — a YouTube link, with your yt-dlp found. Today, with no yt-dlp, this card offers a 37.1 MB download.

Y1Nothing new: straight to the video

MY PICK. The card looks the link up and shows the title, exactly as with Forge’s copy. Your yt-dlp is never mentioned because nothing is asked of you.

Y2The card’s tag says whose

The tag on the card’s edge reads YouTube · your yt-dlp. Tells you no download happened, every time — which you may not care about after the first.

OPTIONS — your yt-dlp is older than the one this Forge is tested with (2026.08.19). Known before anything runs.

O1Offer Forge’s copy

The card offers the download as today. Just works; costs the 37.1 MB this round is trying to save, and leaves your old copy old.

O2Say how to update yours

The card names the one command. No download, your copy gets fixed for everything else you use it for — but you have to leave Forge to run it.

O3Refuse, as a refused path is

No card: the E5·b row under the field, red on the tint, with the command. Treats it as an error, which it is not quite.

O4Both ways on the card

MY PICK. The free fix in the card (the command), the paid one on ↵ (Forge’s copy, its size on the key). You choose space or convenience.

O5Forge runs the update for you

With your OK, ↵ runs brew upgrade yt-dlp and shows its clock. Easiest — but Forge runs your package manager, which can take minutes and update other things too.

O6Don’t check, just try

No version check at all: an older yt-dlp often still works. Only if it fails does Forge say anything (tab 4). Fewest words; the failure comes later, after the wait.

OPTIONS — your yt-dlp ran and could not fetch the video, most likely because YouTube changed. Today: Cannot transcribe this link · yt-dlp could not fetch this video: Sign…

F1Today’s row, the fix as its reason

MY PICK. Same one-line refusal; the reason becomes the command. yt-dlp’s own words go (they were cut off anyway).

F2The full panel: cause, then fix

The E5 panel under the field, home making room: what YouTube said, then the command in the panel’s grey. Room for both, at the cost of a bigger red block.

F3Offer Forge’s copy instead

The card stays and offers Forge’s copy, which updates itself when YouTube breaks it. Gets you the video now; costs the 37.1 MB.
