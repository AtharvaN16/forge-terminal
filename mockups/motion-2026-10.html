Motion

Seven animations, and they're the only ones. Each is a change of a character or colour in a cell, redrawn at no more than 30 fps, so all of them can be built in Ink. The picker demos use the band with a strip (B2) from the picker explorations.

  1. Motion answers "is something happening?" or "what changed?" It's never decoration. After the launch sweep, the idle screen doesn't move.
  2. Keys never wait. Anything a keypress triggers finishes in 150 ms or less, and the next keypress cuts it short.
  3. Loops run only while real work runs: measuring a size, or converting. They stop when the work stops.
  4. No fabricated progress (invariant 7). Converting shows the true phase and the elapsed time. The size bars animate only towards a size that has already been measured.
