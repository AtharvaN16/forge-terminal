Colour mode, simpler — Flow 1

Round 4 with every pop-up as a window in the middle of the screen, as Mode and Tweak are today. The photo flow comes next.

What changed

  1. Home stays. S Scale opens the Colour tab; D Palette opens the Palette tab of the same screen.
  2. Four tabs: Colour · Neutral · Alpha · Palette.
  3. A More button on the screen holds Gradient, Tweak and Steps (Mode on Palette). Its list and what it opens are windows in the middle of the screen, as Mode and Tweak are today.
  4. Gradient takes as many colours as you like.
  5. Today’s words stay. New ones: More · Palette (as a tab) · Gradient (the bar and menu; today “Mix”) · add · remove
