/extensions — Forge and its extensions

The first split is Forge itself against its extensions. Four ways to draw it under the list, and the saving from using your own copy.

What changed

The split is now Forge itself against its extensions. Forge 0.4.5 takes 34.5 MB on disk, measured on this Mac (most of it the image library and the PDF engine). The extensions take 52.0 MB: Boost 14.9 MB, Remove background 32.6 KB, yt-dlp 37.1 MB; ffmpeg is your own, so it takes nothing of Forge’s. Forge is the light grey; inside the extensions, the tool the cursor is on is gold.
