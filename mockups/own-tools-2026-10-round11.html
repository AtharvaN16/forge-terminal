5Rules

  • Forge looks for yours by itself, in the places your terminal looks.
  • Using yours removes Forge’s copy. If yours goes, Forge offers its download again.
  • Only for tools you could have yourself: yt-dlp, ffmpeg. Boost and Remove background keep today’s panel.
