/extensions — the space they take

Five ways to show it under the list, over the real screen at real sizes.

What this is

Under the list in /extensions, how much space Forge’s tools take. Real sizes: Boost 14.9 MB, Remove background 32.6 KB, yt-dlp 37.1 MB; ffmpeg is your own (Homebrew), so it takes nothing of Forge’s. In every option the tool the cursor is on lights in the menu’s gold; the rest stay grey.

References: WhatsApp · Manage storage (total, bar, legend) · Google One and SoundCloud (one bar over sized rows) · PlayStation (each item’s own size).

Words to approve

“on this Mac” (A, E) · the short names Boost, Cutout, yt-dlp, ffmpeg (A–C) · “yours” (A, B, D) · “Total” (B, D) · “a square a MB” (C) · “Forge’s tools”, “saved by yours” (E).

OPTIONS — the space the tools take, under the list. Cursor on YouTube links; its part lights gold. Pick one.

AA total, one bar, a legend

WhatsApp’s storage screen: the total first, one bar split by tool, then each tool’s size. Reads top-down: how much, what of it, which.

BA bar per tool

Each tool its own bar against the biggest, its size at the end, the total under them. Easiest to compare two tools; no legend to match colours against.

COne square a megabyte

A waffle: 52 squares for 52 MB, the cursor’s tool in gold. The size becomes something you can count; 32 KB still gets its one square.

DJust the numbers

No graphic: each list row says its size where the ✓ was (“yours” for your own copies), a rule, and the total. The quietest; the list itself is the chart.

AND — once you use your own yt-dlp

EWhat yours saved

Ties to using your own tools: Forge’s bytes solid, the space your own yt-dlp saved hatched in green beside them. Goes with any of A–D, or on its own.
