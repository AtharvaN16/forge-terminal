Several files at once

Compress, convert and edit take several files, or a folder, and do each one. The CLI always could. Drawn from the app’s own screens at 80 × 24.

Questions for you

  1. Pick one Done: A, B, C, D or E (the “Done for several files” tab). The buttons on the flow and edit/pdf screens are for anything you want changed; I’ve made those calls.
  2. New words to approve: 2 not saved (the panel’s tab, or the row), can’t get below 612 KB, can’t be read, it’s damaged, one image at a time (Crop to shape), Edit 12 images, esc stop on Working.
  3. Decided without asking (say if any is wrong): a folder in convert still watches; a folder in compress or edit is a batch of its files; images and videos mixed in edit aren’t one batch; mixed kinds in convert and compress ask each kind’s setting, as a mixed folder does.

G1Edit, twelve images

Every row applies to each image, except Crop to shape: where to crop is a choice per picture, so it is one image at a time. Trim doesn’t dim: an image with no empty edges is left as it is and told at Done. Boost: an image too large for the scale is left as it is.
