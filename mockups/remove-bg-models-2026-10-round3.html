When to say "no subject"

  • Rule: if the model's top score is under 0.5, there is no subject. The score is almost always 0 or 1 (768: 36 photos near 1, 6 near 0, none in between), so any limit from 0.01 to 0.98 gives the same answers. 0.5 is the safe middle.
  • Plus: if the cut-out would cover under 1% of the photo, there is no subject. Apple refuses a wall photo where 768 marks a few leaf specks (0.2% of it). The smallest real subject in this set covered 3.5%, so anything from 0.3% to 3.4% works; I picked 1%.
  • With both rules, 768 agrees with Apple on 7 of its 9 refusals. The two it misses are the glass ball and a ploughed field. Neither can be told apart from a real small subject by the model's score or size.
