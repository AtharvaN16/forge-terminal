Your questions from round four

  • Does N5 only appear once a file is staged? Yes — and only when at least one preset can run on it. Idle home, or a file no preset fits, keeps today’s field.
  • Does Forge pick the presets by the kind of file? Yes. It reads what the file is from its contents (never its name), and p lists only the presets that can run on it — photo presets for a photo, video presets for a video, and for a folder each preset says how many of its files it fits. (Today a preset can only be made from an image; video and sound presets would be new.)

Instead of ticks in the list: your steps along the top as grey pills, and the list holds only what you can still add.

Instead of ticks in the list: two columns — your steps and Done on the left, what to add on the right; ←→ between them.
